Hello, I'm Bruna


I'm passionate about technology, learning new things, and building cool projects.


This is my little corner of the internet. It is not a portifolio, it wasn't made to get me a job. It was made so I can have some sort of sovereignty over the stuff that I produce.


I think technology in general, not only the ones that we use in the digital world, is the amazing culmination of human ingenuity, curiosity and group effort. For me is so beautiful that we all collectively got together and with a lot of effort created the internet! Or that I am able to create this website because HTML exist and there are a bunch of people showing me how to use it. Or that a boat can sail through the whole ocean.


I have an extreme passion to learn how things work, be it http servers or how the economy of the United Kingdom is organized or how the brazilian coalition goverment can survive the rise of fascism in the world.


I'm passionate about how this digital world can be made to help lives get better.


And I like to build things that I know will be function and have a meaning. This meaning can be just to be a fun thing to use (or play) or this meaning can be "we are able to deploy 100 machine learning models in a metter of days because this workflow is better" or "the resilience and reliability of this integration gave us more time to work on tech debts". I'm just really happy on working on things that last and that I know it works well for other people.


That is one of the reasons that I try my best to not rely on LLMs. They can be useful, yes, but at the same time they are not reliable at all, they make more mistakes than humans do. The only good point is that they are faster, but is that a good point? The "move fast, break things" era of digital technology is over, nobody (besides a handful of really rich detached from reality guys) benefited from it. Now it's time for "do good things that last and help people".


Slow and steady wins the race
